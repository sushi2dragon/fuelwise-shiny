What changes your fuel bill?

Compare how vehicle weight and horsepower affect estimated fuel economy, then see what that could mean for your annual fuel budget.
You enter FuelWise shows
Vehicle weight and horsepower Estimated MPG and a 95% prediction interval
Annual mileage and price per gallon Estimated annual fuel cost

The model draws on 32 cars in the historical mtcars dataset.

One scenario, calculated in R

3,000 lb 110 hp 12,000 miles per year $4 per gallon

model <- lm(mpg ~ wt + hp, data = mtcars)
scenario <- data.frame(wt = 3, hp = 110)
mpg <- unname(predict(model, scenario))
annual_cost <- 12000 / mpg * 4
data.frame(MPG = round(mpg, 2),
           Annual_fuel_cost_USD = round(annual_cost, 2))

   MPG Annual_fuel_cost_USD
1 22.1              2172.07

FuelWise divides annual miles by estimated MPG to calculate gallons used.

How much can MPG vary?

The 95% prediction interval describes uncertainty for an individual car with the chosen weight and horsepower.

plot of chunk interval

The data describe 1970s cars. Use the model to explore relationships in that sample.

Try a comparison

  1. Open FuelWise and start at 3,000 lb and 110 hp.
  2. Increase the weight and compare the estimated MPG and annual cost.
  3. Use the observed-data plot and built-in help to interpret the result.
